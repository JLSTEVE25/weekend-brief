🍷 FRIDAY EVENING
Supperland
After a sweet kindergarten play this morning, Friday night is yours — and Supperland in Plaza Midwood is the move. The restored church setting, wood-fired steaks, and Michelin-recognized cocktail program make it one of Charlotte's best date nights, full stop. Fog rolling in just adds to the atmosphere of tucking into that dramatic space.

Olde Mecklenburg Brewery (OMB)
Saturday morning is a full sports gauntlet — Cam's soccer and t-ball back to back, then Will's soccer in the early afternoon. By 2:30 everyone deserves to decompress, and OMB's massive biergarten is the perfect landing spot. The kids can run the playground while you grab a Copper lager; with only an 11% rain chance it should be comfortable outside, and you're back home well before John heads to the Charlotte FC match.
