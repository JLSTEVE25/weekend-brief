⚽ Crown & Crown at Bank of America Stadium
Charlotte FC hosts Houston Dynamo Saturday night at 7:30 — grab the boys and make it a family night downtown. Will and Cam will lose their minds for the pyro and the Crown section energy. Arrive early to snag a good spot and grab food before kickoff.
SATURDAY EVENING
🎬 Indoor movie marathon with the boys
Saturday's calling for thunderstorms with a 41% rain chance and a high of 99 — not exactly backyard weather. Pull the shades, stack up some pillows, and let Will and Cam pick the double feature. Perfect excuse to do absolutely nothing and call it intentional.
SATURDAY AFTERNOON
🥞 Sunday morning brunch run
Sunday opens up completely, and while it's still drizzly (high of 78), it's the coolest day of the weekend by far. Hit Easy Like Sunday or Dogwood with the whole family — both do weekend brunch right and can handle a table of four. Get there early before the wait stacks up.
